# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: Next.js App Router, already in the repository, deployed on Vercel. The redesign keeps that stack.

## Users

A person who wants a piece from a family jewelry house and has not walked into the shop yet. They need to understand what Joyería López is and leave a commission.

## Product Purpose

The first public door of Joyería López. Success is a visitor who can name the house, see how a piece is commissioned, and leave what they are looking for.

## Positioning

The page is the counter before the door. The piece is commissioned in a conversation. There is no published street yet, so the name is the address.

## Operating Context

Spanish. Family house in Bolivia. Metals named in the house: oro 18k, plata 925, piedras, and work made to order. The catalog on the page is a sample until the real pieces replace it.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The commission form stays on the page and does not send mail or WhatsApp until a destination exists. Address, hours, phone, email and Instagram are empty until they are written in `src/data/site.ts`. No prices, no customer quotes, no founding year.

## Brand Commitments

The name is Joyería López. The voice is direct Spanish, spoken as the house would speak to someone at the counter. The user asked for a high-jewelry page and left the visual world to this redesign.

## Evidence on Hand

Copy and sample piece names live in `src/data/catalog.ts` and `src/data/site.ts`. There are no photographs of the house or of its real pieces. Pictures of the trade come from open collections and are credited. They are not presented as the house's own work.

## Product Principles

- Say only what the house can stand behind.
- The commission is the action, not a catalog checkout.
- Sample pieces stay labeled as muestra.
- Missing contact facts stay missing until the family supplies them.
